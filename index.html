Scope of work

What’s included

End-to-end delivery from design and content preparation through launch and handover.

01

UI/UX design

Website structure, visual direction, page layouts, typography, colour application and user journeys aligned with the Fiscal Stories brand.

02

15-page website

Design and development of up to 15 agreed pages using a consistent, responsive design system and reusable page components.

03

Content writing

Website copywriting for the agreed pages based on the client brief, business inputs and approved positioning.

04

Blog & case study audio

Audio listening option on blog and case study pages, subject to the selected implementation and available browser/device support.

05

Responsive development

Layouts optimised for common desktop, tablet and mobile screen sizes, with cross-browser checks on current major browsers.

06

SEO-ready foundations

Page titles, meta descriptions, semantic heading structure, image alt text, XML sitemap and robots.txt configuration where applicable.

07

AWS hosting & CDN

Website hosting and content delivery network configuration on AWS for one year, subject to the agreed traffic and usage limits.

08

Contact & enquiry tools

Contact form with email notifications, click-to-email and click-to-call links, and WhatsApp contact link where details are provided.

09

Performance optimisation

Image compression, sensible asset loading and basic page-speed optimisation to support a smooth browsing experience.

10

Analytics readiness

Integration of client-provided Google Analytics 4 and Google Search Console properties, where access and tracking IDs are supplied.

11

Quality assurance

Pre-launch checks for links, forms, responsive layouts, page rendering and key user journeys, followed by agreed fixes.

12

Launch & handover

Deployment to the agreed domain, handover of agreed access and a walkthrough covering routine website updates.

Scope note: The final list of 15 pages will be mutually confirmed before design begins. Any additional pages, major new functionality or scope changes will be estimated and approved separately before work starts.
